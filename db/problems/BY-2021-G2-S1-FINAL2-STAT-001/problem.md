## Q
$P(A)=\dfrac{1}{3},\ P(B^{c})=\dfrac{3}{4},\ P(A\cup B)=\dfrac{1}{2}$일 때, $P(A\cap B)$는?

## Choices
① $\dfrac{1}{6}$
② $\dfrac{1}{8}$
③ $\dfrac{1}{10}$
④ $\dfrac{1}{12}$
⑤ $\dfrac{1}{14}$

## Answer
④

## Solution
$P(B)=1-P(B^{c})=1-\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.
따라서
$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cup B)
=\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{12}
$$
이므로 정답은 ④이다.
