## Q
$\log_6(3\sqrt2)-\log_6\sqrt3$을 계산하면?

## Choices
① $-1$
② $0$
③ $\dfrac12$
④ $1$
⑤ $2$

## Answer
③

## Solution
\[
\log_6(3\sqrt2)-\log_6\sqrt3
=\log_6\left(\frac{3\sqrt2}{\sqrt3}\right)
=\log_6\sqrt6
\]
\[
=\log_6(6^{1/2})=\frac12
\]
이므로 정답은 ③이다.
