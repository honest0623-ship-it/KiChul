## Q
식
\[
\frac{\sqrt[3]{3}}{\sqrt[3]{81}}
\times\left(\sqrt[4]{9}\right)^2
\div\sqrt[4]{\sqrt{6^8}}
\]
을 간단히 한 것은?

## Choices
① $\dfrac16$
② $\dfrac13$
③ $2$
④ $3$
⑤ $6$

## Answer
①

## Solution
\[
\frac{\sqrt[3]{3}}{\sqrt[3]{81}}
=\frac{3^{1/3}}{3^{4/3}}=\frac13,\quad
\left(\sqrt[4]{9}\right)^2=3
\]
\[
\sqrt[4]{\sqrt{6^8}}
\;=\;(6^8)^{1/2\cdot1/4}=6
\]
따라서
\[
\frac13\times3\div6=\frac16
\]
이다.
