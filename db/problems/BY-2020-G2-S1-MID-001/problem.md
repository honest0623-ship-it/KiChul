## Q
$\sqrt{\sqrt{128}}\times\sqrt[4]{32}+\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{250}}{\sqrt[3]{2}}$의 값은?

## Choices
① $11$
② $12$
③ $13$
④ $14$
⑤ $15$

## Answer
③

## Solution
\[
\sqrt{\sqrt{128}}\times\sqrt[4]{32}
=128^{\frac14}\times 32^{\frac14}
=2^{\frac74}\times 2^{\frac54}
=2^3=8
\]
또,
\[
\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{250}}{\sqrt[3]{2}}
=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{250}{2}}
=\sqrt[3]{125}=5
\]
따라서
\[
8+5=13
\]
이므로 정답은 ③이다.
