## Q
다음 중 옳지 않은 것은?

## Choices
① $\log_3 5\cdot \log_5 3=1$
② $2\log_2 \sqrt{6}-\log_2 \dfrac32=2$
③ $\log_3 \sqrt{2}+\log_3 \dfrac13-\dfrac32\log_3(\sqrt[3]{6})=\dfrac32$
④ $\log_2 4\cdot \log_4 5\cdot \log_5 8=3$
⑤ $2^{\log_5 3}=3^{\log_5 2}$

## Answer
③

## Solution
③의 왼쪽은
$$
\frac12\log_3 2-1-\frac32\cdot \frac13\log_3 6=\frac12\log_3 2-1-\frac12(\log_3 2+1)=-\frac32
$$
이므로 옳지 않다.
