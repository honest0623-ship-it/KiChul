## Q
$\log_3\left(2-\dfrac12\right)+\log_3\left(2-\dfrac23\right)+\log_3\left(2-\dfrac34\right)+\cdots+\log_3\left(2-\dfrac{52}{53}\right)$의 값은?

## Choices
① 1
② 2
③ 3
④ 4
⑤ 5

## Answer
③

## Solution
각 항의 진수는 $\dfrac32,\dfrac43,\dfrac54,\ldots,\dfrac{54}{53}$이므로
$$
\log_3\left(\frac32\cdot\frac43\cdot\frac54\cdots\frac{54}{53}\right)=\log_3 27=3
$$
