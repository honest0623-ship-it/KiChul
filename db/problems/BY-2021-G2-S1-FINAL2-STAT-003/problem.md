## Q
$P(A)=\dfrac{2}{3},\ P(B)=\dfrac{3}{5},\ P(A\cup B)=\dfrac{13}{15}$일 때, $P(A\mid B)$는?

## Choices
① $\dfrac{6}{7}$
② $\dfrac{5}{6}$
③ $\dfrac{4}{5}$
④ $\dfrac{3}{4}$
⑤ $\dfrac{2}{3}$

## Answer
⑤

## Solution
$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cup B)
=\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{3}{5}-\dfrac{13}{15}=\dfrac{2}{5}
$$
이므로
$$
P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
=\dfrac{\frac{2}{5}}{\frac{3}{5}}=\dfrac{2}{3}
$$
이다.
