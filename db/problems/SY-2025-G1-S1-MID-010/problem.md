## Q
다음 값은?
\[
\sqrt{-3}\left(\sqrt{-27}+\frac{\sqrt{-4}}{\sqrt{3}}\right)
\]

## Choices


① -11
② -1
③ 1
④ 11
⑤ 13

## Answer
①

## Solution
$$
\sqrt{-3}=i\sqrt3,\quad
\sqrt{-27}=3i\sqrt3,\quad
\sqrt{-4}=2i.
$$
따라서
$$
\sqrt{-27}+\frac{\sqrt{-4}}{\sqrt3}
=3i\sqrt3+\frac{2i}{\sqrt3}
=\frac{11i}{\sqrt3}.
$$
그러므로
$$
i\sqrt3\cdot\frac{11i}{\sqrt3}
=11i^2=-11.
$$

따라서 정답은 ①이다.
$$
\sqrt{-3}=i\sqrt3,\quad

$$

$$
