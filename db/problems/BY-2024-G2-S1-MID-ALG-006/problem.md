## Q
$\sin\frac{7\pi}{6}-\cos\frac{11\pi}{3}+\tan\left(-\frac{9\pi}{4}\right)$의 값은?

## Choices
① -2
② -1
③ 0
④ 1
⑤ 2

## Answer
①

## Solution
$\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{6}=-\dfrac12$, $\cos\dfrac{11\pi}{3}=\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{3}=\dfrac12$, $\tan\left(-\dfrac{9\pi}{4}\right)=\tan\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=-1$이다.
$$
-\frac12-\frac12-1=-2
$$
