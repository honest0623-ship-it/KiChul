## Q
$5^a=2$, $5^b=3$일 때, $\log_3 12$를 $a$, $b$로 나타내면?

## Choices
① $\dfrac{2a+b}{a}$
② $\dfrac{a+2b}{a}$
③ $\dfrac{a+b}{b}$
④ $\dfrac{2a+b}{b}$
⑤ $\dfrac{a+2b}{b}$

## Answer
④

## Solution
\[
a=\log_5 2,\qquad b=\log_5 3
\]
이므로
\[
\log_3 12=\frac{\log_5 12}{\log_5 3}
=\frac{\log_5(2^2\cdot 3)}{\log_5 3}
=\frac{2\log_5 2+\log_5 3}{b}
=\frac{2a+b}{b}
\]
이다.
