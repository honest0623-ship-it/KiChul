## Q
연립부등식
\[
\begin{cases}
x^2-2x-15\le0 \\
x^2-2kx+k^2-4<0
\end{cases}
\]
의 해가 \(-3\le x<0\)일 때, 실수 \(k\)의 값은?

## Choices
① \(-3\)
② \(-2\)
③ \(-1\)
④ 1
⑤ 2
## Answer
2

## Solution
The first inequality gives
\[
x^2-2x-15=(x-5)(x+3)\le0
\Rightarrow -3\le x\le5.
\]
The second inequality gives
\[
x^2-2kx+k^2-4=(x-k)^2-4<0
\Rightarrow k-2<x<k+2.
\]
The intersection is given as \(-3\le x<0\), so the second interval must be \((-4,0)\).
Hence
\[
k+2=0 \Rightarrow k=-2.
\]
So the correct choice is 2.
