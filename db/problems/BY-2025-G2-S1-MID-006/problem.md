## Q
$\sqrt{6+2^{\frac52}}\times\sqrt{6-2^{\frac52}}$의 값은?

## Choices
① 1
② $\sqrt{2}$
③ 2
④ $2\sqrt{2}$
⑤ 4

## Answer
③

## Solution
$$
\sqrt{6+2^{\frac52}}\sqrt{6-2^{\frac52}}
=\sqrt{36-\left(2^{\frac52}\right)^2}
=\sqrt{36-32}
=2
$$
이다.
