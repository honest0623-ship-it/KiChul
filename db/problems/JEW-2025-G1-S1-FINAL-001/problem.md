## Q
\({}_5P_3\)의 값은?

## Choices
① 20
② 30
③ 40
④ 50
⑤ 60
## Answer
5

## Solution
\[
{}_5P_3 = 5 \times 4 \times 3 = 60
\]
So the correct choice is 5.
