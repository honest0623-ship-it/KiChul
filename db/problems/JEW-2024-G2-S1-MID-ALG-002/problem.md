## Q
$\sqrt{3\sqrt[3]{9\sqrt[4]{27}}}$을 $3^k$의 꼴로 나타내었을 때, 상수 $k$의 값을 구하면?

## Choices
① $\dfrac{23}{24}$
② $\dfrac{25}{24}$
③ $\dfrac{7}{12}$
④ $\dfrac73$
⑤ $\dfrac12$

## Answer
①

## Solution
$27^{1/4}=3^{3/4}$이므로
$$
\sqrt{3\sqrt[3]{9\sqrt[4]{27}}}=\left(3\cdot (3^2\cdot 3^{3/4})^{1/3}\right)^{1/2}=\left(3^{1+\frac{11}{12}}\right)^{1/2}=3^{23/24}
$$
